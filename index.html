Beviito is a Johannesburg-based DJ and producer known for his dynamic presence in the Afro House, Deep House, and Tech House scenes. He's worked club nights, private events, and the odd rooftop that shouldn't have speakers this good. With a growing discography and a distinctive sound, he's been making waves in the South African electronic music landscape. His musical journey includes releases like "The Calling" and its remix by BK YATRA, alongside tracks like "Los!ng U," "Favours," and "Bout Us." He's graced the decks for some prominent brands over the years, including Truth Nightclub, 5FM, UJFM and Rage Festival.
